🏙️ Destination Guide

Kolkata – The City of Joy

Kolkata, formerly Calcutta, is the capital of West Bengal and the cultural capital of India. A Ranchi to Kolkata taxi drops you right in the heart of this historic city, where colonial-era architecture meets modern India. From the majestic Howrah Bridge and the Victoria Memorial to the trams of Shyambazar and the bookstalls of College Street, Kolkata is a city that rewards exploration.

Top attractions in and around the city include the Victoria Memorial, a white marble museum built in memory of Queen Victoria; Howrah Bridge, one of the busiest cantilever bridges in the world; Park Street, the city's legendary food and nightlife hub; Dakshineswar Kali Temple on the Hooghly; Science City, Asia's largest science centre; and the iconic Indian Museum, India's oldest museum. Business travellers heading to the financial district of BBD Bagh and Salt Lake Sector V use our outstation taxi service regularly.

The best time to visit Kolkata is between October and March, when the weather is pleasant and the city celebrates its most spectacular festival — Durga Puja. While trains from Ranchi take 9–11 hours and buses even longer, a private Ranchi to Kolkata cab covers the 440 km in just 8–9 hours with doorstep pickup and drop — the most comfortable and time-efficient way to travel between the two cities.
