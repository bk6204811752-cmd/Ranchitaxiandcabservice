About Our Ranchi to Hazaribagh Cab Service

Hazaribagh — meaning Thousand Gardens in Persian — is one of Jharkhand's most picturesque and historically significant cities. Perched at 615 metres elevation on the Chota Nagpur Plateau, Hazaribagh enjoys a pleasantly cooler climate than Ranchi and is a favourite weekend retreat for Ranchi residents. The city is renowned for Hazaribagh Lake, Canary Hill viewpoint, and the sprawling Hazaribagh Wildlife Sanctuary which hosts leopards, hyenas, sambar deer, and over 250 bird species.

Our Ranchi to Hazaribagh cab service is among our most popular short-distance routes. The 100 km journey via NH-33 through Ramgarh is smooth and takes just 2 hours. Hazaribagh is also an important educational hub (Vinoba Bhave University) and administrative centre, making this route busy with students, government staff, and business travellers.

We also offer a very popular Ranchi Airport (Birsa Munda Airport) to Hazaribagh direct cab for passengers who need to travel directly from their flight to Hazaribagh without changing vehicles in Ranchi city.

Hazaribagh – A Nature Lover's Weekend Getaway

Hazaribagh is only a 2-hour drive from Ranchi, yet it feels a world away — cool air, forested hills, and quiet lakes make it Jharkhand's favourite weekend escape.

🌊
Hazaribagh Lake

A calm, beautiful lake surrounded by forested hills. Perfect for a peaceful morning walk, boating, and family picnics on the banks.

🦌
Hazaribagh National Park

Part of the Rajiv Gandhi Orang National Park area, this sanctuary shelters leopards, hyenas, sambar deer, and more than 250 bird species.

⛰️
Canary Hill

The highest point near the city offers sweeping sunset views over the Chota Nagpur Plateau and the town of Hazaribagh below.

🌲
Wildlife Sanctuary

Over 180 sq km of dense sal and bamboo forest, ideal for nature walks, bird watching, and spotting wildlife in the early morning.

The best time to take a Ranchi to Hazaribagh cab is between October and March, when the weather is cool and the skies are clear. Winter mornings by Hazaribagh Lake are especially memorable, and the Canary Hill viewpoint glows gold at sunset. Even in summer, Hazaribagh's higher elevation keeps the temperature several degrees cooler than Ranchi, making it a reliable escape from the heat.
