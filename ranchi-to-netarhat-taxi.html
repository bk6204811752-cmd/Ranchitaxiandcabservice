🌄 Places to Visit

Top Attractions Near Netarhat

Netarhat is a nature lover's paradise — best enjoyed with a comfortable cab from Ranchi.

🌅
Netarhat Sunrise Point

Witness the most spectacular sunrise in Jharkhand from the famous Sunrise Point (also called Upper Ghanghri) — worth every early morning effort. Located 10 km from Netarhat town.

🌇
Magnolia Point (Sunset)

Also called the Sunset Point, Magnolia Point offers a breathtaking panoramic view of the surrounding valleys as the sun dips below the horizon. A romantic and serene spot.

💧
Lodh Falls (Burhaghaagh)

At 143 metres, Lodh Falls (also called Burhaghaagh Falls) is one of the highest waterfalls in Jharkhand. Located ~60 km from Netarhat — a stunning detour through dense sal forests.

🐘
Betla National Park

The famous Betla (Palamau) Tiger Reserve is approximately 25 km from Netarhat. Home to tigers, elephants, leopards, and 174+ bird species. A must-visit on any Netarhat trip.

🌲
Upper Ghanghri View

The pristine Upper Ghanghri viewpoint surrounded by dense forest cover offers one of the cleanest and most peaceful experiences in Jharkhand's plateau landscape.

🏫
Netarhat Residential School

Home to the famous Netarhat Residential School — one of India's most prestigious residential schools, established in 1954 amidst the serene forests of Chota Nagpur.

💡 Travel Tips

Ranchi to Netarhat Trip Planning

  • ⏰ Start Early: Leave Ranchi by 4:00–5:00 AM to catch the famous sunrise at Netarhat (arrive by 7:30–8:00 AM).
  • 📸 One-Day Trip: A round trip to Netarhat in one day is possible. Leave at 4 AM, explore Sunrise Point, Magnolia Point, and return by evening.
  • 🏨 Overnight Stay: For Betla National Park + Lodh Falls, a 2-day trip is recommended. Book round trip cab so driver waits.
  • 🛣️ Road Condition: Roads via Lohardaga are mostly good. The last 30 km (Lohardaga to Netarhat) has winding forest roads — thrilling but safe.
  • 📶 Network: Mobile signals are weak at Netarhat. Inform family before entering the forest zone.
  • 🌡️ Clothing: Carry warm clothes even in summer — Netarhat mornings can be quite cold at 1,128 m altitude.

⛰️ Destination Guide

Netarhat — Queen of Chota Nagpur

Perched at an altitude of roughly 3,700 feet on the Chota Nagpur plateau, Netarhat has earned its title of the "Queen of Chota Nagpur". Its cool air, vast pine forests and rolling hills make it a complete escape from the summer heat of Ranchi. Unlike crowded hill towns, Netarhat is calm, clean and uncrowded — the kind of place where the loudest sound is the wind moving through the pines.

Start before dawn at Netarhat Sunrise Point, where the sun rises over a sea of mist, then drive to Netarhat Dam for a peaceful picnic. Continue to the thunderous Lower Ghaghri Falls and finish the day at Magnolia Point as the sun sinks into the valleys below. Walking trails through the pine forests connect most of these spots, and with a private ranchi to netarhat cab you can cover them all at your own pace without waiting for anyone.

Summer is the best time to visit if you want to escape the heat, while the winter months from October to February offer crisp mornings and crystal-clear skies. A private taxi beats the bus because there is no direct Netarhat service from most cities, and it beats the train because Netarhat's small station has limited connections. Booking a ranchi to netarhat taxi gives you a driver who knows the forest roads, so you can simply relax and enjoy the scenery.
