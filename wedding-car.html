Wedding Car Booking FAQs

Is the wedding car fare per day or per hour? ▼
Our wedding packages are priced per day — a standard day package covers 8 hours with an included kilometre limit (80 km for sedans, 100 km for the Innova Crysta and Tempo Traveller). For shorter ceremonies that only need the car for 3–4 hours, we can offer an hourly rate on request.
How many hours does a day package include? ▼
Every day package includes 8 hours of service within Ranchi. Extra hours are charged at a small per-hour rate, and extra kilometres are billed at a fixed per-km rate that is clearly shared with you at the time of booking.
Do you provide wedding cars outside Ranchi? ▼
Yes. We provide wedding cars for weddings in Bokaro, Jamshedpur, Dhanbad, Hazaribagh, Deoghar and other parts of Jharkhand. Outstation wedding packages include the driver's stay, with the extra kilometres and charges explained clearly before you confirm.
Can we book multiple cars for guests? ▼
Absolutely. For guest pickups and big baraat processions, we arrange any number of cars — sedans, Ertiga, Innova Crysta and Tempo Travellers — all decorated uniformly. Fleet bookings of 5 or more cars receive a special discounted rate.
What happens if the wedding car breaks down on the wedding day? ▼
We guarantee a backup car on every wedding booking. If any vehicle develops an issue, a decorated replacement is dispatched to your venue immediately, so your wedding schedule is never delayed.
