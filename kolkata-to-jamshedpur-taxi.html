🏙️ Destination Guide

Jamshedpur – The Steel City of India

Jamshedpur, widely known as Tata Nagar, is Jharkhand's largest city and the home of Tata Steel — the city that gave India its first steel plant. Founded in 1907 by Jamsetji Nusserwanji Tata on the banks of the Subarnarekha River, Jamshedpur is one of the few Indian cities planned on a grid layout, with wide tree-lined avenues, roundabouts, and a reputation as one of the cleanest and most livable cities in the country. A Kolkata to Jamshedpur taxi drops you right in the heart of this green industrial township.

Top attractions in and around the city include Jubilee Park, a 120-acre garden built on the site of an old steel plant dump that now hosts a zoo and a Japan-style temple; Dimna Lake, a popular picnic spot near the Dalma Wildlife Sanctuary; and the Tata Steel Zoological Park, home to big cats, monkeys and reptiles. The city is also home to prestigious institutions like XLRI (Xavier School of Management), one of India's top B-schools, and NIT Jamshedpur, a leading engineering institute. Business travellers heading to the Tata Steel Works, TCS offices and Tata Motors facilities use our outstation taxi service daily.

The best time to visit Jamshedpur is between October and March, when the weather stays pleasant for sightseeing and the annual Tata Steel Marathon and Christmas illuminations at Jubilee Park draw visitors from across the state. While trains on the Kolkata–Jamshedpur section take 6–8 hours and state buses from Kolkata even longer, a private Kolkata to Jamshedpur cab covers the 290 km in just 5–6 hours with doorstep pickup and drop — the most comfortable and time-efficient way to travel between the two cities.
