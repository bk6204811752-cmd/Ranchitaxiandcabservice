✈️ About Birsa Munda Airport, Ranchi

Birsa Munda Airport (IXR) — Ranchi's Gateway

Birsa Munda Airport (IATA: IXR) is the primary international airport serving Ranchi, Jharkhand, and the surrounding region. Located at Hinoo, Khokambatoli — just 7 km from Ranchi city centre — it is named after the legendary Adivasi freedom fighter and tribal icon Birsa Munda. The airport was upgraded to an international terminal in 2012 and now serves domestic flights to Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Chennai, Patna and international connections.

The airport is a critical transport hub for Jharkhand's government officials, corporate executives, tourists visiting Jharkhand's tribal and nature destinations, and the thousands of pilgrims heading to sacred sites like Deoghar (Baidyanath Dham) and Parasnath (Shikharji). Our office is located at Birsa Munda Airport — giving us the most direct access for passenger pickups of any taxi service in Ranchi.
