⛰️ Destination Guide

Parasnath Shikharji — The Spiritual Heart of Jharkhand

Why travellers from Ranchi, across India, and around the world take a cab to Giridih.

Giridih is one of Jharkhand's most spiritually significant districts, and at its heart stands Parasnath Hill (Shikharji) — the highest peak in Jharkhand at 1,365 metres and the most sacred pilgrimage site in the Jain religion. It was on this very hill that Lord Mahavira, the 24th and last Tirthankara, attained Kevala Jnana (absolute knowledge) and later nirvana. The summit area hosts 27 Tonks (Jain temples), one dedicated to each Tirthankara, making it a destination every Jain devotee aspires to visit at least once in a lifetime.

Thousands of devotees from Ranchi, Kolkata, Patna, and abroad travel to Giridih every year, many of them undertaking the challenging 26 km trek up to Shikharji. Madhuban, at the foot of the hill, is the base point where trekkers begin their ascent. Our drivers know the Madhuban foot point route perfectly and can drop you there in the morning and pick you up after your trek — so you never have to worry about finding transport in a new town.

Giridih is not just about Shikharji. The district also offers Usri Falls, one of Jharkhand's most beautiful seasonal waterfalls; Khandoli Park, a scenic green getaway; and the historic mica mine regions that once made Giridih world-famous. The best time to visit is October to March, when the weather is pleasant for both trekking and sightseeing. For pilgrims planning a same-day darshan, an early-morning cab from Ranchi is the smartest choice — you reach Madhuban by 10 AM, complete the trek, and return the same evening.
