📖 Our Story & Journey

A Ranchi-Born Cab Company That Grew

Ranchi Taxi And Cab Service was founded in 2014 with a simple but ambitious idea: every passenger travelling in and out of Ranchi deserved a taxi service they could genuinely trust. We began our journey at Birsa Munda Airport with just three vehicles, a handful of dedicated drivers, and one promise — arrive on time, charge honestly, and treat every traveller with respect. There were no apps, no booking portals, and no marketing budget back then. Our growth came purely from satisfied customers who recommended us to family, colleagues, and friends.

Word spread quickly across the city. Families heading to Ranchi city markets, corporate teams flying into Birsa Munda Airport, tourists exploring Jharkhand's waterfalls and hills, and pilgrims visiting Deoghar and Baidyanath Dham all began relying on us. Today our fleet has grown to more than 30 well-maintained AC vehicles — Swift Dzires, Ertigas, Innova Crystas, and Tempo Travellers — managed by a professional office team based in Hinoo, Ranchi. Every car is serviced on schedule, GPS-tracked, and cleaned before each trip.

Over more than a decade, we have completed more than 50,000 rides across Ranchi, Jharkhand, and neighbouring states. Our vehicles now cover routes to Dhanbad, Jamshedpur, Bokaro, Hazaribagh, Deoghar, Giridih, Patna, and Kolkata. Through every single journey, our focus has never changed: be the best taxi service in Ranchi by simply being reliable, honest, and consistent — trip after trip, year after year.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Your Questions, Answered

Everything you need to know about booking a taxi in Ranchi with us.

How do I book a taxi with you?▼
Booking is simple — call or WhatsApp +91 90061 23451 with your pickup point, destination, date and time. Our office confirms your driver and fixed fare within minutes, and for advance bookings we recommend reserving at least two hours before departure.
Do you operate 24 hours?▼
Yes, we are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including Sundays and public holidays. Early-morning flights landing at Birsa Munda Airport and late-night emergency trips are handled by our night-coordination desk.
Can I get a GST invoice?▼
Absolutely. Corporate travellers and businesses receive a proper GST invoice for every trip on request. Just share your company name and GSTIN when booking, and the bill will be generated after the journey.
Do you provide airport pickup with meet & greet?▼
Yes. For Birsa Munda Airport arrivals, our coordinator waits at the exit holding a name board, helps with luggage, and escorts you to a pre-cooled cab. We track your flight and adjust the pickup time automatically if it is delayed.
How do I pay for my cab?▼
We accept cash, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), and card payments after the trip. For corporate clients we support monthly billing with GST invoices, and for long outstation packages a small advance can be paid by UPI to confirm the booking.
