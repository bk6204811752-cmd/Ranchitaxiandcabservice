Outstation Cab FAQs

What is the minimum booking for outstation cab from Ranchi? ▼
For outstation bookings, there is a minimum of 300 km per day (or approximately ₹3,300 for sedan). This covers driver allowance and vehicle costs for long-distance travel.
Are toll charges included in the outstation fare? ▼
Yes. Our one-way fixed outstation fares are all inclusive — driver, fuel, vehicle, toll and the route distance are all covered in one price. There are no extras at the toll booth.
Can I book a cab for multi-city outstation tour from Ranchi? ▼
Yes! We specialize in multi-city tours. For example: Ranchi → Deoghar → Patna → Gaya → Ranchi. We create custom itineraries with per-day fixed pricing.
Is night driving allowed for outstation from Ranchi? ▼
For passenger safety, we follow a policy of no driving after midnight on outstation routes. For overnight journeys, we plan overnight stays at safe locations.

Every outstation cab from Ranchi is billed on a minimum of 300 km per day, which is the industry standard for long-distance car hire. If your actual run stays under 300 km for the day, the fare is still calculated on the 300 km minimum, because the driver's return leg, vehicle depreciation and his time are all part of the outstation cost. That is exactly why a one-way drop like Ranchi to Dhanbad is quoted as a fixed point-to-point fare rather than a simple per-kilometre charge.

Tolls, state entry taxes and inter-state permits are included in our all-inclusive one-way fares on Jharkhand routes — driver, fuel, vehicle, toll and the route distance come in one price with no hidden charges. On interstate routes like Ranchi to Patna, Ranchi to Kolkata or Ranchi to our driver carries the required inter-state permit documents, so there is never a hold-up at the checkpoint.

For long routes beyond 400 km, a small driver allowance (bhatta) is added to cover the night halt, meals and rest the driver needs before heading back. On one-way trips where the driver returns empty, the fare already accounts for the return run. Before you confirm, our team gives you a single itemised quote — so the final outstation taxi fare from Ranchi never surprises you.
